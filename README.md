## Project Overview

Wanjiku operates a fresh-produce stall at Gikomba Market and wants to understand her business performance using two weeks of daily sales and stock records. Her main challenges include determining whether sales are growing, identifying the best and worst sales days, understanding weekday and weekend performance, and deciding which products need restocking. This project uses core Python to analyse 14 days of sales data, calculate business performance metrics, classify daily sales, compare weekly performance, evaluate product stock levels and calculate gross margins. The findings provide practical recommendations to help Wanjiku improve inventory management, increase sales and make better business decisions.

## Top Three Findings

1. **Business Growth:** Total sales over 14 days were KES 46,450. Week 2 sales increased to KES 24,400 from KES 22,050 in Week 1, representing 10.66% growth.

2. **Weekend Performance:** Average weekend sales were KES 4,900 compared to weekday average sales of KES 2,685. Weekend sales were approximately 82.50% higher.

3. **Stock Management:** Tomatoes and Sukuma require immediate restocking because their current stock levels of 18 kg and 9 kg are below their reorder levels of 25 kg and 15 kg respectively.
